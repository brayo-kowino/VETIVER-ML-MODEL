The Engineering Context

Why Vetiver & The Core Challenge

Nature's green retaining wall meets data-driven computational decision support

🌿 The Bio-Engineering Power of Vetiver
  • Massive Root Network: Grows 2.5 to 3.5 meters deep within 12–18 months.
  • Root Tensile Strength: Averages 75–85 MPa (~1/6 of mild steel reinforcement).
  • Extreme Tolerance: Highly tolerant to drought, heavy metals, flooding, and salinity.
  • Cost-Effective: Up to 70% cheaper than conventional concrete gravity retaining walls.
⚠️ The Real-World Bottlenecks
  • Uncertain Failure Boundaries: High salinity, impenetrable bedrock, or arctic freeze causes site failure without early warning.
  • Scattered Field Literature: Crucial empirical trial findings remain buried in academic PDF papers.
  • Remote Accessibility: Field technicians and farmers do not have complex desktop GIS tools on-site.
Engine 1 • Predictive Analytics

CatBoost Predictive Suitability Engine

Mathematical classification of site viability with explainable machine learning

🎯 What the Engine Does

Ingests 5 core geotechnical site parameters (Soil Type, Climate, Watering Regime, Salinity, Intended Application) and outputs:

  • Binary Verdict: SUITABLE vs. UNSUITABLE.
  • Statistical Confidence Level: Precise probability rating (e.g., 94.2%).
  • Risk Classification: Safe Match vs. Failure Risk.
💡 Why We Selected CatBoost
  • Native Categorical Handling: Eliminates one-hot dimension explosion on non-numerical geotechnical labels.
  • Robust on Small/Medium Data: Built-in ordered boosting prevents overfitting.
  • Native TreeSHAP Integration: Provides immediate mathematical attribution of which factor drove the verdict.
  • Sub-Millisecond Inference: Instant classification on standard CPU hardware.
Engine 2 • Knowledge Intelligence

RAG Literature & Research Assistant

Natural language geotechnical Q&A powered by Qwen 2.5 3B & LlamaIndex

🔍 The Retrieval-Augmented Architecture
  • LLM Engine: Qwen 2.5 (3B parameters) via local Ollama.
  • Embedding Model: nomic-embed-text (high-dimensional semantic vectors).
  • Vector Store: LlamaIndex with persistent local storage in storage/.
  • Prompt Engineering: Enforces scientific geotechnical tone without robotic dataset preambles.
📚 Enterprise Capabilities
  • Zero Hallucinations: Answers strictly grounded in peer-reviewed field observations.
  • Source Attribution: Displays matched paper filename, similarity score, and exact text snippet.
  • Fast Local Execution: Optimized for 6 CPU threads, concise token limits, and permanent RAM residency.
