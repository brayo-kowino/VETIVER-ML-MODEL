# VETIVER GRASS (CHRYSOPOGON ZIZANIOIDES) EMPIRICAL GROWTH, BIOENGINEERING & REMEDIATION REGISTER

## Scientific Overview
This register provides the definitive empirical evidence base of 124 positive global and regional field studies demonstrating the quantitative performance of *Chrysopogon zizanioides* across geotechnical slope stabilization, sediment erosion control, mining and heavy metal phytoremediation, riverbank revetment, and wastewater treatment.

---
